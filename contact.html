Safety guidance

  • Never send passwords, full authentication tokens, financial details, medical records, or government identification.
  • Do not put highly sensitive personal information into the AI assistant.
  • For immediate danger or urgent real-world safety concerns, contact a trusted adult or local emergency service rather than relying on this website.
  • AI output should be checked before it is used for important decisions or submitted as schoolwork.

Response expectations

Messages are reviewed as availability permits. A response time is not guaranteed. Sending an email does not create a professional, legal, or emergency-services relationship.
