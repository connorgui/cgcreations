Classic Snake

Steer the snake around the grid, eat the food to grow longer, and keep moving without hitting a wall or your own body.

State Ready
Controls

Arrow keys or WASD to steer. Space pauses or resumes. Press R to restart.

Game Board

One food equals one point and one extra segment.

Status

Press Start Game or use arrow keys or WASD.

How to Play

The snake moves one square at a time in the current direction. Every food pickup adds a point, and the run ends if the head crosses the boundary or touches the snake body.
