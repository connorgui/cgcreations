About the project

Small tools built to make learning feel lighter.

CG Creations is an independent project that brings focused study practice, school organization, voice experiments, memory challenges, and strategy games into one responsive website.

Why CG Creations exists

The goal is to make useful digital experiences that are quick to understand and comfortable on phones, tablets, and computers. The collection grows through experimentation with design, programming, learning mechanics, and interactive tools.

What you will find

  • Reading, writing, and math practices with generated variations.
  • A homework tracker with optional account synchronization.
  • Games with local and computer-controlled opponents.
  • Number, prime, Pi voice, and memory tools.
  • A Groq-powered Learning Assistant for explanations and practice.

What CG Creations is not

It is not a school, tutoring service, medical service, or professional advice provider. The AI assistant and generated exercises can make mistakes. Users should verify important schoolwork with a teacher, textbook, or trusted source.

How trust is approached

The site publishes its data practices, offers optional analytics controls, uses hashed passwords and secure session cookies, provides account deletion, and names third-party services involved in running features.
