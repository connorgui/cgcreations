Cookie Policy

Essential storage is separate from analytics.

You can use the site with visitor analytics disabled. This page explains cookies and browser storage used by CG Creations.

Current storage

Name or categoryTypePurpose
cg_sessionEssential HttpOnly cookieKeeps a signed-in account authenticated. It uses SameSite=Lax and lasts up to approximately 30 days.
cg_cookie_preferences_v1Essential local storageRemembers whether optional visitor analytics are allowed.
cg_terms_choice_v1Essential local storageRemembers whether the first-visit Terms notice was accepted, declined, or dismissed.
Remembered username and UI choicesFunctional local storageUsed only when requested to remember a username, dismiss a prompt, or save supported display preferences.
Local game recordsFunctional local storageKeeps certain best scores or progress on the current browser.
Visitor analyticsOptional server recordAfter permission, records IP address, first-seen time, and country to count unique visitors. It is not an advertising cookie.

How consent works

Before a choice is made, visitor registration is disabled. Choosing “Essential only” keeps it disabled. Choosing “Allow analytics” permits one visitor-registration request from that browser session. You can change the preference at any time below.

Clearing browser data

Browser settings can remove cookies and local storage. Doing so signs you out, removes locally saved preferences and records, and causes the Terms and cookie notices to appear again. Clearing browser data does not automatically delete an account or server records; use Delete Account or contact the site for that.

Do Not Track

Browsers do not provide a consistent, universally supported Do Not Track standard. The site's own privacy controls are the reliable way to disable optional visitor analytics.
